Our story

Seven years, two cities, one very patient dog.

We met in the queue for the last train out of the city on a rainy Tuesday. The train was cancelled. We shared a taxi, then a coffee, and then most of the next seven years.

How we met

Arjun lent Maya his umbrella and never got it back. She still says it was a fair trade for the directions she gave him to the wrong platform.

The proposal

On a cold morning at Willow Lake, during a walk that Arjun insisted had to start at 6 a.m. Maya said yes before he finished the question.

Travel & stay

Sonoma is about an hour's drive north of San Francisco.

Flying in

San Francisco (SFO) and Oakland (OAK) are both about 75 minutes away. Sonoma County Airport (STS) is 30 minutes away with direct flights from several West Coast cities.

Questions
Anything else, just ask us.

    

      

        Can I bring a plus-one?
        
Your invitation lists everyone we've saved a seat for. If you'd like to bring someone, please ask us first and we'll do our best.

      

      

        Are children welcome?
        
We love your little ones. Children are welcome at the welcome evening and the brunch. The ceremony and reception are adults only.

      

      

        What will the weather be like?
        
Sonoma in March is mild during the day, around 18°C (65°F), and cool in the evenings. The ceremony is indoors in The Glasshouse; bring a layer for the lawn.

      

      

        Do you have a gift registry?
        
Your presence is the gift. If you'd like to give something, a contribution to our honeymoon fund will be at the welcome evening.

      

      

        Can I take photos during the ceremony?
        
We're having an unplugged ceremony, so please keep phones away until we've walked back down the aisle. Snap away at the reception.
